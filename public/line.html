The line cut

Six questions on one revenue line — how it earns, what it truly contributes, and where it goes next — plus the contract facts that cannot be inferred. About eight minutes, answered by whoever owns the line. This produces three domain reads and one or more lowest candidates, not an index: lines are compared with lines, never with brands.

The line

Name the brand exactly as its own assessment named it, so the two connect. Built for licensed or contracted lines; for an owned line, answer for the line's own economics and mark the contract fields not applicable.

Line identity and evidence

These fields connect this cut to the same governed commercial line used by the brand assessment. They are descriptive and do not change the scores.

The contract facts

Captured, never scored — a fact is not a maturity. These are what let the read-out say when the terms are next open, whether the income is verified, and whether the mark is protected where the line sells.
